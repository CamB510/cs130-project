## How I Would Answer My Questions

To find which states have the highest average SAT scores, I would use Python to go through the school scores dataset and group the SAT scores by state. I would calculate the average SAT score for each state and then compare the averages. Finally, I would sort the results from highest to lowest to see which states have the highest SAT scores.
